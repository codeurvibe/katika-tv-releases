# KATIKA TV

A player for Fire TV and Android TV, for live TV, movies and series from your own provider. You need your own Xtream Codes login or M3U link; the app doesn't come with one.

## Install on a Fire TV Stick

1. On the Home screen, search for **Downloader** and install it.
2. Go to **Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Install Unknown Apps** and turn it on for Downloader. If you don't see Developer Options, go to **Settings → My Fire TV → About**, press OK seven times on your device's name, then go back.
3. Open Downloader and enter this code in the URL address bar : **3221626**  or type : https://github.com/codeurvibe/katika-tv-releases/releases/latest/download/katika.apk
4. Choose **Install** when the download finishes, then open KATIKA TV.
5. Enter your login and tick the countries you want for live TV.

On other Android TV devices, the steps are the same: install Downloader from the Play Store and allow it to install apps when asked.

## Updating

The app doesn't update itself. To get a newer version, repeat step 3. It installs over your current version and keeps your login and favorites.

## Using the remote

| Where | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Lists | OK | Open or play |
| Lists | Menu (≡) | Add to or remove from favorites |
| Lists | Left | Open the side menu |
| Live TV | Up / Down | Change channel |
| Live TV | Rewind | Back to the previous channel |
| Live TV | Left / Right or Menu | Options: channel number, picture shape, audio, subtitles |
| Movies and series | OK or Play/Pause | Pause or play |
| Movies and series | Left / Right | Skip 10 seconds; hold to go faster |
| Movies and series | Down or Menu | Show controls, then picture shape, audio and subtitles |
